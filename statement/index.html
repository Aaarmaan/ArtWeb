STATEMENT

ARMAN MAHMOUDI

Figures accused, who do not know why. They wait, not knowing what for. The empty space around them is neither shelter nor escape, a silence heavier than any wall. The cold color of their bodies and the centerless stains behind their heads cut them off from the world. No court is visible, yet it fills every painting.
